# Data documentation

- All records are fictional course data. No real residents, service records, or facilities are represented.
- `service_requests.geojson` contains five point requests; R-05 lies on the shared boundary of the supplied service areas.
- `service_areas.geojson` contains two adjacent service polygons. `planning_districts.geojson` contains two overlapping planning polygons for the overlay exercise.
- Coordinates are GeoJSON CRS84 / WGS 84 (EPSG:4326). The shapes are simplified and illustrative.
- Do not use these data to make a real service or planning decision.
